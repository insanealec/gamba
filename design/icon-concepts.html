Icon concepts

Six directions for the favicon and installed-app icon, each checked against a light browser tab, a home-screen tile, and the real in-app header next to GAMBA. so the pick works everywhere it'll show up, not just at one size.

The monogram

Just the existing wordmark, cropped to its own initial. Same cyan-and-magenta-dot treatment already in the header — nothing new to learn, reads as "Gamba" instantly for anyone who's seen the app once.

The chip

A poker chip's edge spots, redrawn in gold with a cyan center. The most literally "casino" of the set — instantly legible as gambling, at the cost of being the direction least specific to Gamba itself.

The crash graph's line — climb, peak, drop — with the cash-out point marked. The one option pulled from Gamba's own mechanics rather than generic casino iconography, though it leans on players already knowing that game.

The dice

Two overlapping dice in the app's magenta and cyan. Playful and colorful, but the most generic "gambling app" shorthand of the set — the shape a dozen other casino apps could also use.

The token

A faceted credit token — four flat facets in the app's own neon pairing, gold-outlined like a stamped chip. Reads as "value" without being a real coin or a real casino chip, which fits a currency that's explicitly fake.
